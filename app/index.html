Input device
Let the tap hear the music too

  1. Open Audio MIDI Setup (Applications → Utilities)
  2. Click + → Create Multi-Output Device
  3. Tick your speakers and BlackHole — speakers listed first
  4. Tick Drift Correction on BlackHole only
  5. System Settings → Sound → Output → pick the new device

macOS disables the volume keys on a Multi-Output Device — use Spotify's own volume.
